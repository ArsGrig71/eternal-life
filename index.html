Eternal Life is a Free Gift

Eternal life is a gift. It is not earned or deserved. No amount of personal effort, good works, or religious deeds can earn a place in heaven for you. For a gift to benefit you—you have to receive it.

We all deserve judgment and death, yet God offers you eternal life — as His free gift. The Lord Jesus died on the cross to pay for your sins. He rose again from the dead. He can give you, at the very moment you trust Him, the free gift of eternal life.

For the wages of sin is death; but the GIFT of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.Romans 6:23

Eternal life is found in a Person. The Lord Jesus Christ. The Lord Jesus offers this gift to you. Take it. Simply place your trust in Him. The big question is: will you receive His gift of salvation? Here’s how:

Believe

Accept Christ’s payment for your sin, and put your trust in Him, with a believing heart. Trust the Lord Jesus Christ as your Saviour. Receive His peace and forgiveness. Peace with God.

Therefore being justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ.Romans 5:1

God’s Forever Gift

The Lord Jesus shows us God’s love, and gives us eternal life.

And this is the record, that God hath given to us eternal life, and this life is in his Son. He that hath the Son hath life; and he that hath not the Son of God hath not life. These things have I written unto you that believe on the name of the Son of God; that ye may know that ye have eternal life…1 John 5:11–13

If you believe in the Lord Jesus Christ, the Bible says that you can know that you have eternal life — forever — it can never be lost!

And I give unto them eternal life; and they shall never perish, neither shall any man pluck them out of my hand.John 10:28
